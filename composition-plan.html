Issue badge Displays the story category. Appears in the header. Absolute positioning places it in the corner of the header. The feature header is the containing block. Moves to the left side of the header on smaller screens. Check the badge at different screen sizes.
Story guide Links readers to story sections. Appears before the article. Sticky positioning keeps it visible while scrolling. The story guide stays within its grid area and scrolling container. Sticky positioning is used only on wider screens. Test scrolling and section boundaries.
Pull quote Highlights an important dining custom. Appears between paragraphs. Relative positioning and a negative margin create an overlap. The quote is positioned within the article and uses the raised layer token. Returns to normal flow on smaller screens. Check that text and content are not covered.
Feature image Shows an example of Japanese food culture. Appears inside a figure with a caption. Clip path creates subtly rounded image corners. The image is inside the feature figure. Without clip path support, the image remains visible with square corners. Test with clip path disabled.
Festival image Illustrates Japanese festivals and traditions. Appears before the related paragraphs. Border radius creates a circular image. No additional containing block is needed. The image remains visible without rounded corners. Check the image at different screen sizes.
Festival image text wrap Allows text to flow around the image. Image and paragraphs remain in normal document order. Float, shape outside, and shape margin create circular text wrapping. The float affects text flow within the article section. Without shape outside support, the image remains a regular float and text wraps around its rectangular shape. Test with shape outside disabled.
